# Echo-of-Maren

**Role:** Side Character Showcasing the Race  
**Race/Faction:** The Inheritors  
**Archetype:** Absorbed human pilot, still partially herself  
**Public Face:** Wry, wounded, unstable  
**Private Fear:** That she is being used as proof the collective is kinder than it is

Maren was once a human pilot captured during the early Inheritor escape. Now she exists as an Echo: a preserved personality used for translation, tactical memory, and human psychological modeling.

She is not fully free, but not fully erased. She can argue with the collective. She can remember her old crew. She can also deploy Inheritor organisms with terrifying calm because part of her now believes in the collective's survival.

Maren is the best side character for making the Inheritors uncomfortable rather than merely scary. She can explain them, condemn them, defend them, and contradict herself in the same briefing.

**Story Function:** Shows that assimilation is not binary death, but also not clean salvation.

**Conflict Hook:** Human characters may recognize her, forcing questions about rescue, mercy, and whether the person they knew still exists.

**Gameplay Tie:** Introduces mimic tactics, human-derived units, stolen command codes, and missions where identity confusion matters.

**Sample Line:**  
"I remember dying. I also remember deciding not to. Both memories insist they are true."
