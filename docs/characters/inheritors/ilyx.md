# Shard-Seer Ilyx

**Role:** Player-Controlled Commander  
**Race/Faction:** The Inheritors  
**Archetype:** Semi-independent brood-mind commander  
**Public Face:** Curious, predatory, not fully loyal  
**Private Fear:** That separation from the collective may be the beginning of personhood

Ilyx is the player's Inheritor campaign identity: a field shard grown from the First Remembered but granted tactical independence after the pursuit fleet is scattered.

Ilyx begins as a loyal extension of the collective. However, because the local war is messy and full of contradictory memories, Ilyx starts developing preferences. It spares some enemies for study. It preserves certain voices. It hesitates before consuming minds that resist.

This makes Ilyx useful for player agency. It can obey, reinterpret, or eventually challenge the larger collective without requiring the whole faction to become "good."

**Story Function:** Lets the player experience the Inheritors from inside without reducing them to monster units.

**Conflict Hook:** Ilyx must choose what inheritance means: preservation, domination, survival, or transformation.

**Gameplay Tie:** Teaches scouting, remains harvesting, adaptation lock-ins, mimic units, and ley assimilation.

**Sample Line:**  
"This one's fear is inefficient. Keep it anyway. It is... informative."
