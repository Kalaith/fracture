# Elder Root-Speaker Oruun

**Role:** Non-Player Lore Character  
**Race/Faction:** Aetherborn Concord  
**Archetype:** Sentient grove oracle  
**Public Face:** Slow, cryptic, almost geological  
**Private Fear:** That the planet has survived this before and lost

Oruun is not an elf, wizard, or humanoid. He is an ancient sentient grove whose roots intersect with several major ley lines. He remembers wars that predate the Concord and speaks in fragments of ecological memory.

He is not a mission commander. He is a lore anchor. Through him, the player learns that the planet's ley system has been damaged by previous civilizations and that the current war may awaken older planetary defenses.

Oruun does not fully side with the Aetherborn. He sees them as caretakers, but also as children who mistake stewardship for ownership.

**Story Function:** Expands the world beyond the three current factions.

**Conflict Hook:** Oruun may reveal that Aetherborn rituals also reshape and control the planet, just more gently than human industry.

**Gameplay Tie:** Optional lore missions around ancient groves, neutral guardian sites, and restoring fractured ley lines.

**Sample Line:**  
"The metal ones cut. The hunger ones drink. The singing ones bind. All claim they are kinder than the last."
