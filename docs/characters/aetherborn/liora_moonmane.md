# Liora Moonmane

**Role:** Side Character Showcasing the Race  
**Race/Faction:** Aetherborn Concord  
**Archetype:** Unicorn lancer, battlefield healer, reckless noble  
**Public Face:** Bright, brave, teasing, beloved by troops  
**Private Fear:** That all beauty in her world is becoming military infrastructure

Liora is a unicorn-bonded cavalry officer who leads fast harassment and rescue missions. She embodies the elegant, mobile, magical side of the Aetherborn. Her unit is not just cavalry; they ride along ley currents, appearing where the battlefield's living flow allows.

She is charming, but not soft. Her humor has teeth. She grew up in a culture where war songs, clan duty, and ritual duels are ordinary civic life, so she does not see herself as militaristic. She sees herself as properly prepared.

Liora helps humanize the Aetherborn by showing their beauty, pride, and blind spots. She genuinely struggles to understand why humans do not recognize the planet as alive.

**Story Function:** Gives the player an emotionally accessible Aetherborn companion.

**Conflict Hook:** She starts as anti-human, but changes after protecting human children from an Inheritor mimic attack.

**Gameplay Tie:** Introduces mobile ley movement, healing windows, flank attacks, and rescue objectives.

**Sample Line:**  
"You walk across the world as if it were dead stone. No wonder it throws armies at you."
