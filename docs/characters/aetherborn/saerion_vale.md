# Commander Saerion Vale

**Role:** Player-Controlled Commander  
**Race/Faction:** Aetherborn Concord  
**Archetype:** Young battlefield conductor  
**Public Face:** Disciplined, adaptive, respected but not fully trusted  
**Private Fear:** That the old ways are beautiful and insufficient

Saerion is the player's campaign identity for the Aetherborn. He is a ley-war commander trained to treat battlefields as living spell diagrams. Unlike older Concord leaders, he is willing to study human technology and Inheritor behavior instead of dismissing both as profane contamination.

He is not a rebel at the start. He is loyal, sharp, and dutiful. But he gradually becomes the bridge between preservation and adaptation. Some elders consider him dangerously curious. Some younger wardens see him as the only commander who understands the new war.

**Story Function:** Lets the player question Aetherborn orthodoxy without immediately betraying the faction.

**Conflict Hook:** Saerion must choose between perfect obedience to sacred law and battlefield decisions that actually save lives.

**Gameplay Tie:** His missions teach ley node expansion, ritual circuits, mobile defense, and later hybrid tactics against Inheritor corruption.

**Sample Line:**  
"If the enemy changes with every battle, then tradition must become a weapon, not a cage."
