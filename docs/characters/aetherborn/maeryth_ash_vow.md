# Archon Maeryth Ash-Vow

**Role:** Main Villain Within the Faction  
**Race/Faction:** Aetherborn Concord  
**Archetype:** Purist war zealot  
**Public Face:** Hero of the border clans  
**Private Fear:** That coexistence would make every death meaningless

Maeryth is a legendary Aetherborn commander whose clan lands were among the first damaged by human extraction. She now leads the Ash Vow, a militant sect that believes the planet can only be saved through total cleansing of foreign presence.

She is not stupid and not cartoon evil. That makes her more dangerous. She understands the humans are afraid. She simply does not care. To her, fear does not excuse occupation.

Maeryth becomes the internal villain of the Aetherborn campaign because she pushes defensive war into ritual extermination. She is willing to destroy corrupted ley regions even if Aetherborn civilians, captured humans, or neutral spirits are trapped within them.

**Story Function:** Forces the Aetherborn campaign to confront the ugliness of righteous defense.

**Conflict Hook:** She accuses Saerion of weakness whenever he refuses to sacrifice territory or prisoners.

**Gameplay Tie:** Her missions may involve scorched-earth rituals, forced cleansing, and optional objectives where the player can save civilians at strategic cost.

**Sample Line:**  
"A wound is not healed by negotiating with the knife."
