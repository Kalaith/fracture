# Captain Elias Rourke

**Role:** Player-Controlled Commander  
**Race/Faction:** Terran Expeditionary Directorate  
**Archetype:** Field commander caught between orders and reality  
**Public Face:** Competent, loyal, tired  
**Private Fear:** That command is lying to him because they know he would disobey

Rourke is the player's human campaign identity. He leads forward operations from the crash perimeter into contested Aetherborn territory. At first, his job is simple: secure matter, stabilize power, protect civilians, and keep the fabricators running.

He is practical rather than ideological. He does not begin as a critic of occupation. He becomes one because he sees the cost directly: burned groves, frightened Aetherborn civilians, human convoys attacked in revenge, and soldiers asked to defend sites they should never have taken.

Rourke is a good RTS commander because he can believably receive orders, question them, and still execute under pressure.

**Story Function:** Lets the player experience the slide from survival into occupation from ground level.

**Conflict Hook:** Rourke must choose whether to obey Voss, expose hidden Inheritor information, or negotiate with Aetherborn commanders.

**Gameplay Tie:** Teaches base building, battery logistics, convoy defense, forward bases, and tactical evacuation.

**Sample Line:**  
"We keep calling it a perimeter. It stopped being a perimeter three valleys ago."
