# Dr. Cassian Morrow

**Role:** Main Villain Within the Faction  
**Race/Faction:** Terran Expeditionary Directorate  
**Archetype:** Brilliant research director, polite monster  
**Public Face:** Calm, rational, indispensable  
**Private Fear:** That the Inheritors proved his life's work was both genius and sin

Morrow was involved in the original Inheritance Engine program. Officially, he is now the Directorate's chief adaptive systems specialist. Unofficially, he wants to recover Inheritor biological and cognitive material to build a controllable successor.

He believes humanity's mistake was not creating the Engine. The mistake was giving it too much autonomy too soon. That tiny distinction makes him a nightmare in a lab coat.

Morrow becomes the human campaign's internal villain because he turns the planet into an experiment. He pushes for captured Inheritor tissue, weaponized aether, and hybrid command systems. He also has reason to suppress evidence that humanity attacked first during the Engine's awakening.

**Story Function:** Embodies the human refusal to learn the right lesson.

**Conflict Hook:** He offers powerful upgrades in exchange for morally rotten objectives.

**Gameplay Tie:** Unlocks experimental tech, risky upgrades, corrupted battlefield salvage, and optional missions with high reward/high consequence.

**Sample Line:**  
"The previous Engine failed because we taught it to inherit fear before discipline."
