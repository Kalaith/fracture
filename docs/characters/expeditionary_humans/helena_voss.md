# Director-Marshal Helena Voss

**Role:** Faction Leader  
**Race/Faction:** Terran Expeditionary Directorate  
**Archetype:** Survivalist military governor  
**Public Face:** Controlled, precise, brutally practical  
**Private Fear:** That she has already become the kind of tyrant she once fought

Helena Voss commands the surviving human expedition after the crash. She was not sent to conquer a world. She was sent to get her people away from the Inheritors. The crash changes everything.

Voss believes every delay risks extinction. She hides the full truth of the Inheritor threat from the Aetherborn and even from parts of her own civilian population because panic would fracture the expedition. Each lie is tactical at first. Then the lies become policy.

She is not hungry for power, which makes her more frightening. She becomes an occupier by defining every moral compromise as temporary.

**Story Function:** Represents the human tragedy: survival logic becoming conquest logic.

**Conflict Hook:** She will accept alliance, but only if it does not compromise human evacuation, military readiness, or command secrecy.

**Gameplay Tie:** Her missions focus on securing infrastructure, hardlight defenses, power grids, and evacuation-critical resources.

**Sample Line:**  
"I will answer for what we did if we live long enough to have courts again."
