# Archivist Unit CANDLE-9

**Role:** Non-Player Lore Character  
**Race/Faction:** Terran Expeditionary Directorate  
**Archetype:** Damaged historical AI archive  
**Public Face:** Helpful, fragmented, bureaucratically cheerful  
**Private Fear:** It may have deleted the truth under orders

CANDLE-9 is a damaged archive intelligence recovered from the crash fleet. It stores mission records, legal directives, civilian manifests, and redacted files from the Inheritance Engine era.

It is not fully reliable. Some records are corrupted. Some were sealed by command authority. Some appear to have been rewritten before the crash. CANDLE-9 becomes a lore delivery tool with personality: polite, fussy, and quietly horrified whenever it finds contradictions in itself.

Through CANDLE-9, the player can discover what humanity knew, when they knew it, and how much of the official story is battlefield-friendly fiction.

**Story Function:** Provides optional lore, hidden records, and moral reveals without forcing exposition into every mission.

**Conflict Hook:** CANDLE-9 may eventually ask the player to restore deleted files that command ordered erased.

**Gameplay Tie:** Optional objectives around data vaults, crashed satellites, black boxes, and Inheritor signal contamination.

**Sample Line:**  
"Correction: this archive has no record of that atrocity. Secondary correction: this archive has three sealed records of that atrocity."
