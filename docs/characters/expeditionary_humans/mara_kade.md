# Chief Engineer Mara Kade

**Role:** Side Character Showcasing the Race  
**Race/Faction:** Terran Expeditionary Directorate  
**Archetype:** Fabricator chief, logistics specialist, convoy saint  
**Public Face:** Sarcastic, overworked, impossible to intimidate  
**Private Fear:** That every miracle repair extends the war

Mara Kade keeps the Directorate alive. She oversees field fabrication, battery depots, power relays, drone repairs, and the endless parade of "temporary" installations that slowly become permanent occupation infrastructure.

She showcases the human faction's best qualities: ingenuity, grit, speed, improvisation, and the ability to turn wreckage into a functioning base before breakfast. She also showcases their worst: if something can be used, humans will use it.

Mara is not a politician. She measures morality in lives saved this hour. That makes her sympathetic and dangerous.

**Story Function:** Makes the human machine feel human.

**Conflict Hook:** She knows the extraction rigs are damaging the planet, but shutting them down means hospitals, shields, and food processors lose power.

**Gameplay Tie:** Introduces salvage, battery supply chains, deployable structures, emergency repair, and power grid vulnerabilities.

**Sample Line:**  
"Give me six drones, two hours, and something expensive to cannibalize."
