# Fracture Command - Victory Walkthrough Guide

## Victory Condition

**To win:** Control the majority of sectors (3+ out of 5) for 30 consecutive seconds.

If the timer reaches 30 seconds, your faction wins immediately!

---

## Quick Start (First 30 Seconds)

### 1. **Understand Your Commander** (Top-left panel)
- **Vanguard**: +10% damage - Best for aggressive play
- **Control**: +20% morale recovery - Good for sustained fights
- **Disruptor**: +5% damage - Balanced offense

Your commander type affects your passive bonuses throughout the match.

### 2. **Check Your Resources**
- **Supply**: 100/100 (increases to 120 if you control a Supply Depot sector)
- **Spawn Cooldown**: 3 seconds between unit spawns
- Units cost supply based on their type (3-20 per unit)

### 3. **Identify the Sectors** (marked with letters)
Look for sector type indicators:
- **S** = Supply Depot (+20 max supply when controlled)
- **H** = High Ground (+15% damage for units fighting here)
- **F** = Fortified (+20% defense for units here)
- **I** = Industrial (-15% spawn time when controlled)
- **○** = Standard (no bonus)

---

## Early Game Strategy (0-2 minutes)

### Phase 1: Initial Spawn
1. **Press 1-2-3 to spawn your first squads** (keys 1-0 spawn different unit types)
   - Start with **Heavy Infantry (2)** - Tough and versatile (8 supply x3 = 24 total)
   - Or **Light Infantry (1)** - Cheap and fast (3 supply x3 = 9 total)

2. **Target Priority Sectors:**
   - **Supply Depot (S)** first - Gives you +20 max supply = more units
   - **High Ground (H)** second - Your units deal +15% damage there
   - **Standard sectors** third - Easy to capture, count toward victory

### Phase 2: Set Doctrines (Optional but Powerful)
Press **F1** and **F2** to cycle through doctrines for slots 1 and 2:
- **Aggressive Posture**: Units advance faster (more pressure)
- **Shock Windows**: Freshly spawned units deal bonus damage
- **Rapid Deployment**: Spawn units faster
- **Resource Efficiency**: Units cost 15% less supply

**Good Combo**: Aggressive Posture + Shock Windows = +15% synergy bonus!
**Bad Combo**: Aggressive Posture + Entrenched Assault = -15% conflict penalty

## Mid Game Strategy (2-5 minutes)

### Building Your Army

#### Unit Roster (Keys 1-0):
| Key | Unit Type | Cost | Role | Best Against |
|-----|-----------|------|------|--------------|
| 1 | Light Infantry | 3×3=9 | Scout, cheap pressure | Light units |
| 2 | Heavy Infantry | 8×3=24 | Frontline tank | Infantry |
| 3 | Light Tank | 10×3=30 | Mobile damage | Infantry |
| 4 | Heavy Tank | 15×3=45 | Heavy assault | Infantry, sectors |
| 5 | Artillery | 20×3=60 | Long-range siege | Structures, heavy units |
| 6 | Scout Drone | 5×3=15 | Fast recon | Information |
| 7 | Gunship | 12×3=36 | Air support | Ground units |

| 9 | Anti-Armor | 10×3=30 | **2.5x damage vs tanks!** | Armor units |
| 0 | Anti-Air | 10×3=30 | **3x damage vs drones!** | Drones |

### Unit Counter System (Critical!)
- **Anti-Armor destroys tanks** (2-2.5x damage)
- **Anti-Air shreds drones** (2.5-3x damage)
- **Tanks crush infantry** (1.3-2x damage)
- **Artillery punishes static units** (1.4-1.8x damage)

**Example:** If enemy spawns Heavy Tanks, spawn Anti-Armor units to counter them!

### Squad Orders (Select squad with left-click, then Q/E to change)
- **Advance**: Push forward aggressively (default)
- **Hold**: Defend current position
- **Skirmish**: Hit and run tactics
- **Fortify**: Dig in and build defenses

### Strategic Markers (Right-click on sectors)
- **First right-click**: Attack marker (red arrow)
- **Second right-click**: Defend marker (blue shield)
- **Third right-click**: Remove marker

Use these to communicate intent and coordinate with AI allies.

## Late Game Strategy (5+ minutes)

### Morale Management
Watch the colored bars below squads:
- **Green bar** = High morale (1.3x damage)
- **Yellow bar** = Medium morale (1.0x damage)
- **Red bar** = Low morale (0.7x damage)

**Morale drops when:**
- Squad takes heavy casualties
- Units drop below 50% health

**Morale recovers when:**
- Time passes (Engineers recover 50% faster!)
- Squad is out of combat

**Tip:** Pull back damaged squads to recover morale before re-engaging!

### Sector Bonuses in Action
1. **Fighting on High Ground (H)?** Your damage is +15% there!
2. **Defending Fortified (F) sector?** Enemy damage is -20%!
3. **Control Supply Depot (S)?** You can field more units!

### Supply Flow Visualization
- **Green bar** = Healthy supply (0-75%)
- **Yellow bar** = Getting full (75-90%)
- **Red bar** = Supply capped (90-100%)

**When supply-capped:** Sacrifice weak squads or wait for casualties to free up supply.

### Game Speed Controls
- **SPACE**: Pause/unpause
- **-**: Slow down (0.25x min)
- **=**: Speed up (4x max)
- **0**: Normal speed

Use slow-mo to micro-manage critical fights!

## Winning Strategy - Step by Step

### Turn 1: Secure Economy (0:00-1:00)
1. Spawn Heavy Infantry (key 2) x2 squads
2. Send one squad to nearest Supply Depot (S)
3. Send other squad to nearest sector
4. Set doctrine: **Resource Efficiency** (F1, cycle to it)

**Result:** +20 max supply from depot = 120 total supply capacity

### Turn 2: Build Army (1:00-2:30)
1. Spawn units to counter enemy composition:
   - See enemy tanks? → Spawn Anti-Armor (9)
   - See enemy drones? → Spawn Anti-Air (0)
   - See enemy infantry? → Spawn Tanks (3 or 4)
2. Set second doctrine: **Rapid Deployment** (F2)
3. Claim High Ground (H) sector if possible

**Result:** Faster spawns + counter advantage = battlefield control

### Turn 3: Pressure & Expand (2:30-4:00)
1. Use unit counters to win fights efficiently
2. Capture majority of sectors (3+ out of 5)
3. Place Attack markers (right-click) on enemy sectors
4. Pull back squads with low morale (red bars)

**Result:** Control 3+ sectors to start victory timer

### Turn 4: Hold the Line (4:00-Victory)
1. **Victory timer appears** when you hold 3+ sectors
2. Defend your sectors for 30 seconds
3. Use Fortified (F) sectors for defense (+20% armor)
4. Spawn replacement units as squads die
5. Watch the victory timer: "Holding sectors: X/30s"

**When timer hits 30 seconds → YOU WIN!**

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ **Don't**: Spam one unit type
**Why?** Enemy will counter you (Anti-Armor vs your tanks = 2.5x damage!)
**Do Instead:** Mix unit types and adapt to enemy composition

### ❌ **Don't**: Ignore morale bars
**Why?** Low morale = 0.7x damage = you lose fights
**Do Instead:** Rotate damaged squads to rear, let them recover

### ❌ **Don't**: Fight on bad terrain
**Why?** Enemy on High Ground = they get +15% damage
**Do Instead:** Capture High Ground first, force enemy to attack uphill

### ❌ **Don't**: Ignore supply cap
**Why?** Can't spawn units when at 100/100 supply
**Do Instead:** Capture Supply Depot (S) for +20 max supply

### ❌ **Don't**: Use conflicting doctrines
**Why?** Aggressive + Entrenched = -15% penalty
**Do Instead:** Use synergistic combos (Aggressive + Shock = +15%)

## Advanced Tips

### 1. **Commander Abilities Stack**
Your passive ability (e.g., Vanguard +10% damage) stacks with:
- Doctrine bonuses
- Sector bonuses (High Ground +15%)
- Morale bonuses (up to +30%)
- Counter bonuses (up to +150%)

**Best case:** Vanguard Anti-Armor on High Ground with high morale vs enemy tanks
= 1.1 × 1.15 × 1.3 × 2.5 = **~4.1x damage!**

### 2. **Doctrine Timing**
- **Start of game:** Resource Efficiency (build army cheaper)
- **Mid game:** Aggressive Posture + Shock Windows (pressure enemy)
- **Late game:** Entrenched Assault (defend sectors)

Change doctrines with F1/F2 to adapt!

### 3. **Engineer Value**
Engineers (key 8) don't deal much damage, but:
- They recover morale 50% faster (Engineer commander type)
- They support other squads
- They're cheap (8 supply)

Use them to stabilize damaged squads!

### 4. **Industrial Sector (I) Spam**
If you control Industrial sector (-15% spawn time):
1. Your spawn cooldown is faster
2. You can flood the map with units
3. Overwhelm enemy with numbers

**Capture Industrial early** for production advantage!
